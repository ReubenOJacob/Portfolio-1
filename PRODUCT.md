# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Four audiences, in priority order, all evaluating credibility fast:

1. **Investors and partners** deciding within a minute whether Reuben is a serious founder worth a meeting.
2. **Enterprise clients** of Syphon Labs assessing whether the person behind the product is someone to bet a pilot on.
3. **Press and speaking organizers** verifying stature and pulling bio material.
4. **Recruiters and hiring managers**, a secondary audience; the resume must remain findable but must not define the site.

Context: daytime, laptop or phone, arriving from LinkedIn, a pitch email, or a search. They skim before they read.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Reuben Jacob, Co-Founder & CEO of Syphon Labs. Its job is to establish founder-grade authority: lead with the company (flagship product Talenry, the AI career agent; Daisy Recruiter for hiring teams) and the thesis, back it with a real operator track record (NYC Health + Hospitals, NYU, Cognizant, published research), and make contact effortless. Success: a visitor leaves believing this person builds serious things, and knows exactly how to reach him.

## Brand Personality

Quiet authority. Assured, editorial, unhurried. The site of someone who does not need to shout. Three words: composed, substantial, direct.

## Anti-references

- The site's own previous incarnation: animated particle fields, typing-effect headlines, gradient text, glow-on-hover cards, emoji-adjacent playfulness.
- Generic v0/shadcn portfolio look: identical card grids, badge clouds, tabbed skill matrices.
- Pitch-deck loudness: oversized claims, hero metrics, drenched color fields.
- Job-seeker framing: "View Resume" as the primary CTA, certifications as a headline feature.

## Design Principles

1. **The company is the headline.** Syphon Labs and the thesis lead; the biography supports.
2. **Restraint signals confidence.** One typeface family for voice, one accent color, generous whitespace. Nothing animates unless it clarifies.
3. **Track record over claims.** Named institutions, published papers, dated timeline. Let facts carry weight.
4. **Editorial, not app.** The site reads like a well-set document, not a dashboard: text-led, rules and rhythm instead of cards and shadows.
5. **Human, not sterile.** The kept Hobbies section and photography provide warmth; authority without coldness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Honor prefers-reduced-motion (already implemented for remaining transitions). Maintain AA contrast on the ivory paper palette, including the accent on paper. Semantic landmarks and keyboard-reachable nav preserved.
